The Challenge

India’s growth needs young people who can spot problems and build solutions. The Founder’s Grant Challenge invites students from every corner of the country to identify a real problem in Indian society or in their own community, and to propose a practical, original way to solve it.

You don’t need a finished product, funding or experience. You need a clear understanding of the problem, a workable idea and a realistic plan.

Full rules, regulations and terms

01About the challenge
  • The Founder’s Grant Challenge 2027 is organised by Skill Grimoire Private Limited, Bengaluru (“Skill Grimoire”, “we”, “us”). This is the first edition.
  • Teams identify a real problem in Indian society or in their own community and propose a practical, original way to solve it.
  • You don’t need a finished product, funding or experience.
02Who can enter
  • Students currently enrolled in an undergraduate (UG) or postgraduate (PG) programme at a university or college in India, in any stream.
  • Team of 2–4 members. Solo entries are not accepted.
  • All members of a team should study at the same university or college.
  • Each team names one faculty member from their institution as mentor. A faculty member may mentor more than one team.
  • A student can be a member of only one team. Each email address and mobile number can be registered only once; a registration that repeats one is not accepted.
  • There is no limit on the number of teams from one college.
  • We may ask for a college ID card or a letter from your institution at any stage to confirm eligibility.
03How to register
  • Registration is free. There is no fee at any stage.
  • Registration opens on 8 November 2026 and closes on 31 January 2027 at 11:59 pm IST. Late entries are not accepted.
  • The team lead registers once for the whole team, on this page only.
  • After submitting, you get a Registration ID. Quote it in every message to us.
  • All details must be true and complete. To correct anything after submitting, email foundersgrant@skillgrimoire.com from the team lead’s email with your Registration ID before registration closes. Please don’t register again.
04What you submit
  • At registration: your team, member and faculty mentor details, and your sector. No idea or document is needed yet.
  • Prelims: registered teams submit an idea abstract (PDF, 1–2 pages) covering the problem, evidence, your solution, expected impact and your team. We will email how and when to submit it. The prelims are judged on this abstract.
  • Quarter-finals: teams shortlisted from the prelims submit a video pitch of up to 3 minutes. We will email the details. The video is shared as a YouTube (Unlisted) or Google Drive link that anyone with the link can open.
  • Semi-finals: teams present their idea live to the judges online, in batches. The schedule is sent by email.
  • Choose the one sector, out of ten, that best fits your idea.
05Data, sources and AI
  • Any data, figures or statistics in your entry must come from official Government of India or State Government websites, with the source link cited.
  • Data from other sources, such as news sites, blogs, private reports or AI tools, will not be accepted as evidence.
  • You may use AI tools for research and writing. The idea and the plan must be your own, and AI is never a source of data.
06Originality
  • Your entry must be your team’s own original work.
  • Copied or plagiarised entries are disqualified at any stage, including after results are announced.
  • If your idea builds on someone else’s work, say so clearly in your abstract.
07Stages and judging
  • Registration (8 Nov 2026 – 31 Jan 2027) → Prelims (abstract round) → Quarter-finals (3-minute video pitch) → Semi-finals (live online presentations to judges, batch-wise) → Grand Finals (in Bengaluru, date to be announced).
  • Teams compete within their sector until the semi-finals. One finalist team comes from each sector, making 10 finalists.
  • At the Grand Finals, prizes are decided on the strength of the idea and its innovation, not on sector.
  • Entries are judged on Problem Clarity, Solution & Innovation, Feasibility, Impact, and Team & Presentation.
  • Dates for the quarter-finals and semi-finals are shared with shortlisted teams by email.
  • The jury’s decision at every stage is final.
08Prizes
  • The minimum prize pool is ₹3,00,000, and it may grow. All 10 finalist teams receive cash prizes and gifts, and the winners receive Founder’s Grant support to take their idea forward.
  • Prize amounts for each position will be announced before the Grand Finals.
  • Prize money is paid to the team through the process we share with winners, and is subject to applicable tax laws.
09How we use and protect your data
  • By registering, you allow Skill Grimoire to use the details and materials you submit to run the challenge, to contact you about it and about Skill Grimoire’s programmes, and to showcase participants and finalists (names, institution, photos and idea summaries).
  • Where your data is kept: registration details are stored in Skill Grimoire’s Google account (Google Drive and Google Sheets).
  • AI tools: Skill Grimoire uses AI tools, including ChatGPT by OpenAI, to organise, check and manage registrations and to help prepare messages to teams. These tools may process your data on servers outside India.
  • Your data is shared only with the jury, the organising team and the service providers that help us run the challenge, such as Google and OpenAI. We do not sell your personal data.
  • The team lead confirms that every team member and the faculty mentor have agreed to share their details for this challenge.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, email foundersgrant@skillgrimoire.com with your Registration ID.
  • Skill Grimoire may develop submitted ideas in the future, either together with the team or on its own.
10Conduct
  • Be honest and respectful with other teams, the jury and the organisers.
  • False information, misconduct or any attempt to influence the jury leads to disqualification.
11Changes to these rules
  • We may update these rules, dates or the format if needed. Changes are posted on this page and sent to registered teams by email.

Common Questions

Do we need a working product or prototype?

No. You need a clear understanding of the problem, a workable idea and a realistic plan.

Do we need our idea ready to register?

No. Register your team now with your sector, member and mentor details. We will email you how and when to submit your idea abstract for the prelims.

Do we need to make a video now?

No. Registration only needs your team details. Registered teams then submit an abstract for the prelims, and teams shortlisted from the prelims are asked for a 3-minute video pitch for the quarter-finals.

Is registration really free?

Yes. There is no fee at any stage of the challenge.

Can our team have members from different colleges?

No. All members of a team should study at the same university or college, and your faculty mentor should be from that institution.

Can we use AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes, for research and writing. The idea and plan must be your own, and any data must come from official government websites, not from AI.

Can we change our details or sector after registering?

Email foundersgrant@skillgrimoire.com from the team lead’s email with your Registration ID before 31 January 2027. Please don’t register a second time.

How will we know if we are shortlisted?

We contact the team lead by email. Keep an eye on your inbox, and check the spam folder too.

Whom do we contact for help?

Email foundersgrant@skillgrimoire.com, or call or WhatsApp +91 63006 90221.
